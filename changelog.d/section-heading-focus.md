### Behaviour changes

- **Choosing a section in the dashboard's top bar moves focus to the section's heading.** A click
  or Enter on a section link, in the bar or its More menu, focuses the section's (visually hidden)
  heading, so a screen reader announces the section and Tab continues in its content. Loading the
  page, a deep link and Back / Forward leave focus where it is.
  To update: nothing to do; keyboard and screen-reader users land in the section they chose.
