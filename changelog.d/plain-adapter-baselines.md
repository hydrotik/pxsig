### Documentation

- **The plain adapter is checked in pxsig on every change.** [Building a UI adapter](../docs/ui/build-adapter.md#verify)
  says that the plain adapter, the shape an adapter starts from, is rendered on every change in
  both themes, strict and twice. For now the check is advisory: it reports plain's findings
  without failing, until they are fixed and its first baselines are approved.
